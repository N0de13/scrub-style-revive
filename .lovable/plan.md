# New SEO Blog: How Should Scrubs Fit?

## Goal
Add a roughly 2,000-word guide answering **“How should scrubs fit?”** for New Jersey healthcare professionals. This fills a clear content gap: Semrush reports about 210 U.S. searches per month for the exact question with low estimated ranking difficulty (3/100), plus related demand around scrub sizing, tight scrubs, and fit by garment type.

## Content
- Publish **“How Should Scrubs Fit? The Complete Fit Guide for New Jersey Healthcare Professionals.”**
- Open with a concise, answer-first definition suitable for search snippets and voice assistants.
- Include a visible, linked table of contents.
- Cover top fit, pant, jogger, petite/tall, plus-size, movement, dress-code, fabric, fitting-room, and group-uniform considerations.
- Add localized references to Lakewood, Monmouth County, Ocean County, and nearby New Jersey healthcare workers without keyword stuffing.
- Include 7–9 concise FAQs based on real question searches.
- Avoid unsupported claims about specific hospitals’ policies; advise readers to confirm employer dress codes.

## Search & Answer Optimization
- Add a unique meta title, meta description, short keywords, and localized long-tail keywords.
- Add BlogPosting, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList, and Speakable structured data through the existing SEO system.
- Use descriptive headings and direct answers so sections can stand alone in search and AI answers.
- Add the article to the sitemap and `llms.txt` using the real custom domain.

## Internal Links
Link naturally within the article to:
- `/brands` and relevant brand pages such as Barco, Grey’s Anatomy, White Cross, and Landau
- `/embroidery` for names, credentials, and logos
- `/groups` for team sizing and uniform programs
- related articles about joggers, plus-size scrubs, long shifts, and scrub care
- the external online shop only as a clearly labeled shopping option

## Page Experience
- Extend blog content rendering so inline internal links are clickable rather than displayed as bracketed text.
- Add stable section IDs and a styled table of contents with jump links.
- Keep the existing editorial medical-fashion design and mobile behavior.

## Featured Image
Generate a new local watercolor-pencil editorial image showing a diverse group of New Jersey healthcare professionals in well-fitted scrub silhouettes. Use a descriptive local-search-focused alt tag, while keeping the image free of printed words and logos.

## Validation
- Confirm the new post appears first on the blog page and among the homepage’s latest three articles.
- Check table-of-contents jumps, internal links, metadata, structured data, local image loading, and mobile/desktop layout.
- Validate the sitemap XML and run the project’s focused type checks/tests.
